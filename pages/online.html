Online Testing

PARC conducts video-supervised examinations nearly every day of the week. Candidates test from home on their own computers, supervised over Zoom by a team of at least three Volunteer Examiners.

Requirements

  • An FCC Registration Number (FRN), not an FCC username, obtained before booking. The FRN should be registered with an email address other than Gmail. A license cannot be issued without an FRN.
  • Two devices with cameras: a computer for the examination, and a phone or tablet on a stand beside the candidate, at ninety degrees, showing the candidate, both hands and the screen.
  • A Windows or Mac laptop or desktop computer. Chromebooks, iPads and other tablets cannot run the examination.
  • A quiet, private room with the door closed and a clear desk.
  • A physical photo ID, as listed under Acceptable Identification.

From Booking to License

The complete process, in order:

  1. Booking

    Select a time on the schedule. The $15 fee is paid when booking; there is no separate payment step. Use the same email address for every step that follows.

  2. Registration

    A registration link is emailed within 48 hours. Registrations are processed once a day, so candidates who book for the following day should monitor their email, including spam folders. Complete the registration immediately; it is the FCC license application.

  3. Preparation

    Prepare the room, computer, second device and identification, using the pre-exam checklist.

  4. Joining the Session

    The Zoom link is emailed at least 45 minutes before the examination. Join on both devices 45 minutes early and remain in the waiting room until admitted. Sessions may run late; a candidate in the waiting room remains on the schedule.

  5. Check-In

    Present photo identification (requirements differ for candidates under 18), then scan the room slowly with the second camera so that the team can see the entire workspace.

  6. The Examination

    Questions appear on the screen and are answered with the A, B, C and D keys. There is no strict time limit; most prepared candidates finish in approximately ten minutes.

  7. Results

    The team grades the examination during the session and reports the result before the candidate leaves. The CSCE, which records the elements passed, is emailed within 48 hours.

  8. FCC Application Fee

    For a new license, the FCC emails instructions for its $35 application fee. Payment is due within 10 days, or the FCC dismisses the application. Upgrades do not require a fee.

  9. License Issuance

    PARC files electronically, so a license can be granted the same day during FCC business hours. Issuance depends on FCC systems and can take up to 5–7 business days.
