What’s Next – Welcome to Amateur Radio!

Clear instructions and useful links to guide you after passing your exam
    New Licensee Upgrades Restore Your Computer
    Disclaimer: We are not required to provide the following information and links. We do this to help and assist you as we welcome you into the great hobby of Amateur Radio.
    WARNING: Do not make any changes to your records! Do not modify or edit for at least 30 days AFTER you received your call sign or upgrade. ** Do NOT apply for a vanity call sign! **

    New Licensee

  • Pay your $35 license fee as soon as the system allows it. Submit your payment promptly to secure your place in the queue.
  • Watch your email closely. The FCC will send an email to the address listed on your Form 605 once their system begins processing. This email contains the payment instructions. Check your spam and junk folders regularly. Missing this email or failing to pay in time will result in your application being dismissed, and you will have to begin the process again.
  • Felony documentation. If you have a felony conviction, submit the required documentation to the FCC. This process is strictly between you and the FCC — we cannot assist or intervene.
  • Do not contact the FCC, ARRL, or us about your paperwork. The FCC’s public call center is operated by a contractor and may not have accurate information, so please be patient. If you have not received an email within ten days, email us to check on your application for you.
  • How to Pay Your FCC License Application Fee of 35 Dollars Log in and pay as soon as the application is in the FCC system. CORES payment instructions.
  • Check CORES directly, not just your email. Starting two business days after your examination, check your FCC CORES account for your application and the payment option. FCC mail does not reliably reach Gmail, so check Inbox, Spam and Junk — but check CORES itself as well.

When to Expect Progress

  • New licenses are issued in order of payment date.
  • Upgrades and new licenses will process as soon as payment and/or verification steps are complete.
  • You may see your call sign appear in the FCC ULS database within days, or it may take several weeks, depending on the backlog.
  • If you have already paid and do not see your license after ten days, contact the FCC at their toll-free number (888) 225-5322.

Helpful Tips

  • Paying promptly is the best way to avoid unnecessary delays.
  • Keep monitoring email and FCC notices, but do not worry about contacting us or the FCC unnecessarily. It can take up to ten days.
  • Remember that backlogs are normal after system outages and shutdowns. Patience is key.

Congratulations

    You are now a licensed Amateur Radio Operator! The most important thing you can do now is to keep learning and stay active in the community.
      Here are some suggestions to help you get started:
    • Join a local amateur radio club and meet other hams.
    • Attend hamfests and local events.
    • Consider joining the ARRL for resources and advocacy.
    • Avoid buying low-cost handheld radios such as Baofeng or Wouxun, which can be difficult to program and have limited range.
    • Before buying any equipment, learn what others in your area use so you can make informed choices that will serve you for years.
    • ** JOIN PARC ** PARC Radio & Technology hosts a monthly meeting on Zoom with Amateur Radio News and Helpful Tips as well as a special presentation on an interesting topic. Join US in addition to your local club. We would love to have you with us.

After Your Examination — Restore Your Computer

To take the exam, you changed some settings and closed programs you would normally keep running. Put your computer back the way you had it before you return to using it. Start by shutting the computer completely off and turning it back on.

Windows

  • Desktop icons: right-click the desktop → View → Show desktop icons.
  • Do Not Disturb: turn Do Not Disturb or Focus back off.
  • Bluetooth: turn it back on if you normally use it.
  • Browser: close the guest or private examination browser.
  • Restart: restart the computer to reload your normal startup and system-tray programs.

Mac

  • Desktop icons: restore your normal desktop icon display.
  • Stacks: click the desktop → View → uncheck Use Stacks if you normally keep individual icons.
  • Focus: turn Focus or Do Not Disturb back off.
  • Bluetooth: turn it back on if you normally use it.
  • Browser: close the guest or private examination browser.
  • Applications: reopen anything that was force-quit during the exam.
  • Restart: restart your Mac to reload Finder, menu-bar items, and normal background programs.
