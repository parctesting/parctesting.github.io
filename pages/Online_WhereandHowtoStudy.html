Step 1 of 5: Study

Where and How to Study

Book an examination only after consistently scoring 85% or higher on at least 15 practice tests. Each session requires the time of several volunteers and a time slot that could otherwise be assigned to another candidate.

Practice Tests and Classes

HamStudy.org

Free practice tests and a study mode that tracks progress, with an optional low-cost mobile application.

ARRL Exam Review

Free practice examinations from the ARRL, the national association for amateur radio.

New England Sci-Tech

Online license classes recommended by PARC examiners.

Study with Ham Radio Prep

Ham Radio Prep provides an interactive online license course of videos, text lessons, quizzes and games. The course has had more than 60,000 successful students and offers a 100% money-back guarantee to students who do not pass on the first attempt.
