Accessibility Conformance Report

How the PARC website measures against Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, which adopts the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA.

  • Report date: 15 September 2026
  • Product: the PARC examination website, parcradio.org
  • Standards applied: Revised Section 508 (36 CFR Part 1194, Appendix A) and WCAG 2.0 Level AA
  • Contact: vetesting@yahoo.com

This is a self-assessment, not a certification. It reports what PARC has tested. It has not yet been reviewed by an independent auditor, and it has not been tested with screen reader software such as NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver.

Terms Used

  • Supports: the criterion is met.
  • Partially supports: some part of the website does not meet it.
  • Does not support: the criterion is not met.
  • Not applicable: the criterion does not apply to this website.

Parts Outside PARC’s Control

Examinations depend on services that PARC does not build or operate. Their accessibility is set by their own providers:

  • Calendly, where a time is booked and the fee is paid.
  • Zoom, which carries the session.
  • ExamTools, where the examination itself is taken.
  • Study material and forms published by other organisations, such as the ARRL and HamStudy.

A candidate who cannot use one of these should request an accommodation before booking. An examiner can read each question aloud, and other arrangements are listed under Accommodations.

Accommodations

How This Was Tested

  • Every public page, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, at 1280, 768, 390 and 320 pixels wide.
  • Keyboard operation of the menu, search, schedule, forms and the star rating.
  • The browser’s accessibility tree, for the name, role and state of each control.
  • Contrast calculated from the colour values in the stylesheet.
  • Windows High Contrast mode, emulated in the browser.
  • Automated checks that run before every release: broken links, missing new-tab notices, image descriptions, and duplicate identifiers.

Known Limitations

  • No testing with screen reader software has taken place yet.
  • On the schedule, days are shaded to show how many times are open. The shading of a day with few times is close to the page colour, although the number of times is printed in every day.
  • WCAG 2.1 and 2.2, which some state and local government rules now require, have not been assessed in full.
