Schedule Your Exam

Requirements Before Booking

  • An FCC Registration Number (FRN).
  • The same email address must be used to book, pay and register.
  • The $15 examination fee is non-refundable; it is forwarded to the ARRL VEC.
  • Candidates must read the online testing overview and the instructions before booking.

Online Testing Overview Instructions

Date of Birth

Enter your date of birth to display the applicable session times. This step is optional.

Your date of birth remains on your device. It is not sent to PARC or to Calendly and is not stored. It is used once, in your browser, to determine which times to display.

Notices Before Booking

Watch for the FCC application fee email. The FCC allows only 10 days for payment; after that period, it dismisses the application and the examination session is canceled. Once an application has been submitted, PARC examiners generally cannot resolve a missed FCC payment notice. Check spam and junk folders.

Candidates must be fully prepared. Read the instructions and the FAQ before the examination. A candidate who is not ready when admitted is returned to the waiting room, and the next candidate is admitted. A second occurrence requires booking a new examination.

Instructions FAQ

Examination slots begin every 5 minutes. Most candidates finish in approximately 10 minutes; allow 15 minutes for the examination after setup. These figures are for planning purposes and are not time limits. Requests for additional time must be emailed before booking.

After Booking

A registration link is emailed within 48 hours and must be completed immediately. The Zoom link is emailed at least 45 minutes before the examination, and candidates join 45 minutes early. Prepare the room, computer and second device in the meantime.
