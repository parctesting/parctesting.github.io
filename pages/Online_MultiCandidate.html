More than one candidate in the same building

More than one person testing from the same house or building.

Each candidate needs their own booking, their own computer, their own second device for the room camera, and their own room. You cannot share a screen, a desk, or a space — the room scan and camera requirements apply to each person separately, exactly as they would if you were testing alone.

Book each candidate a separate slot on the schedule. Slots at the same time are fine as long as the requirements above are met.
