Step 3 of 5: Prepare

Preparing for Your Session

Four areas must be prepared before the session: the room, the computer used for the examination, the second device, and the candidate’s identification. An examiner verifies all four before the examination begins.

Room

A door that closes, a clear desk, and no other persons present.

Computer

Windows or Mac, one screen, notifications off, and all other applications closed.

Second Device

A phone or tablet on a stand, at ninety degrees to one side.

Identification

Acceptable documents, including the requirements for candidates under 18.

Pre-Exam Checklist

Complete this checklist the day before the examination. An examiner verifies each item before the examination begins, and the session cannot proceed if any item is missing.
