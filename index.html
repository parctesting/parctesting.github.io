Ham Radio License Exams, Online and In Person

PARC is an all-volunteer team of Volunteer Examiners. PARC helped pioneer remote video testing and offers examination sessions nearly every day.

Same-day licensing is possible during FCC business hours, depending on FCC systems.

Welcome

PARC is the longest continually operating online Volunteer Examiner (VE) team in the world. The detailed instructions on this website reflect the experience of tens of thousands of candidates, and the rules exist to protect the integrity of every examination.

Candidates are expected to read the instructions and complete their preparation before the examination; PARC examiners assist with any remaining steps during the session. Candidates with concerns or special needs should contact PARC by email before booking.

Online Exams

Examinations are conducted from home over Zoom by a team of Volunteer Examiners. Careful preparation of the room and equipment is required.

Projects & Mission

PARC is an all-volunteer organization with members throughout the United States, known for administering amateur radio examinations nationwide. PARC is one of the three original groups authorized by the ARRL to pioneer remote video testing for amateur radio examinations.

In addition to examinations, PARC provides training and presentations to community groups, youth groups and scouts. Members volunteer at Hamvention and Hamcation and provide communications and logistics support for community service projects, fundraisers and other events throughout the South. PARC also stands ready to support communities in disaster recovery.

At Auburn University, PARC serves as VE Liaison for the testing sessions of the Auburn University Amateur Radio Club (AUARC), one of the oldest continually operating college radio clubs in the country. PARC also provides the club with training, education and technical support, including improvements to the club station.

With Alpha Phi Omega, a national service fraternity founded on the principles of Scouting, PARC provides merit badge training to hundreds of scouts on campus each year. PARC also assists other scout groups and organizations with JOTA (Jamboree on the Air) through public demonstrations of amateur radio.
